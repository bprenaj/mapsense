# MapSense - Venture Brief

> DRAFT written 2026-08-07 by the company brief loop, from the repo and app as they
> are. Every line is the writer's best inference; edit freely. The OPEN RULING at the

- **Value (one sentence):** Trains your minimap awareness while you play: it watches
  whether you actually glance at the minimap and conditions you with escalating cues
  when you stop checking.
- **Beachhead segment:** Ranked League of Legends players who already own Beam Eye
  Tracker (they have working eye tracking, browse Steam for add-ons, and die to ganks
  the minimap showed them).
- **Why them first:** Zero hardware friction, one-click reachable next to Beam on
  Steam, and map awareness is the most-coached skill gap in MOBA content, so the
  pitch already exists in their feeds.
- **Go-to-market:** Free on Steam next to Beam, plus the Influencer & UGC Flywheel
  aimed at League improvement creators. Overwolf distribution is the second channel
  (docs/monetization-overwolf.md).
- **Traction threshold (draft, owner to confirm):** 1,000 activated installs and 100
  weekly-active trainees within 60 days of the Steam listing.
- **Monetization:** Free with ads (Overwolf owadview + CMP consent); PRO subscription
  gates the gaze-driven coaching and the full MAS + 9 metrics surface (entitlement
  service already in the app). Free mode stays fully functional on timer cues alone.
- **Automation state:** Build, tests, releases, and silent auto-update are CI-run
  (v1.1.4 shipped). Human-run today: Steam/Overwolf listings, creator outreach, the
  ownership ruling below. Next to automate: store listing generation from repo copy.
- **Current gate:** 0 - Define. The app is built well past Gate 1 quality, but a
  venture with an unresolved ownership question has not defined itself yet, so the
  board holds it at the start until the ruling lands.
